# Welcome to YALTI Prompter

Your words. Your voice. This little island at the top of your screen is your teleprompter, and you are reading your very first script right now.

Hover over the island to see the controls. Press the microphone button, or the space bar, and start reading this text out loud. YALTI listens on your own computer and follows along as you speak. [smile]

You do not have to be perfect. Pause whenever you like, skip a sentence, or go off-script to tell a quick story. YALTI waits for you, and when you return to your words, it finds your place again, even if you pick up somewhere else entirely.

## Make it yours

Open your own script with **Ctrl+O**, paste one with **Ctrl+V**, or simply drop a text or Markdown file onto the island. Open the settings with **Ctrl+comma** to choose a font, change the size and colors, and decide how much of the upcoming text you want to see.

Prefer a steady pace instead? Press **M** to switch to auto-scroll, then use the square brackets to go slower or faster. You can always scroll by hand with the mouse wheel or the arrow keys.

## Out of the way

Press **Escape** to fold the prompter into a small island, and click it to bring it back. Drag the island sideways to move it along the top edge, and drag its corner to resize it. When you need it gone, press **Ctrl+Alt+Y** from anywhere, and press it again to bring it back.

Everything stays on your computer. No accounts, no cloud, no recordings. Just you and your words.

Now open your own script, take a breath, and begin.
